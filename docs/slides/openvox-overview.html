The problem

Voice agents are stuck.

Closed SaaS

Fast to start. Expensive forever.

Vapi / Retell / Bland charge per minute, lock you to their providers, and keep your audio on their servers. Margins collapse the moment you scale.

Low-level OSS

Flexible. And months of wiring.

LiveKit Agents / Pipecat give you primitives. You build the dashboard, the templates, the evals, the scheduler, the multi-channel routing — before your first agent says hello.

No middle. Until now.
